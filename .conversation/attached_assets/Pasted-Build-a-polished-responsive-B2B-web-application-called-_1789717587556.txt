Build a polished, responsive B2B web application called **ClaimSignal**.

ClaimSignal is an AI-powered early-warning system for insurance claims operations.

Its purpose is to help claims teams identify open claims that are at risk of escalating into customer complaints or poor customer outcomes, understand why the risk is increasing, and take preventative action before escalation occurs.

This is a portfolio prototype designed to demonstrate AI-native product thinking in insurance, risk, compliance, and claims.

The key product idea is:

**Know which claims are going wrong before your customers have to tell you.**

The core user question is:

**“Which claims need attention today, why, and what should we do next?”**

---

# 1. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Traditional claims systems primarily record what has already happened.

ClaimSignal demonstrates a more proactive workflow:

Claim evolves
→ operational friction appears
→ risk increases
→ system identifies signals
→ AI explains the context
→ preventative intervention is recommended
→ human reviews the recommendation
→ intervention is approved
→ outcome can eventually be measured

The application must feel like credible software used by insurance operations teams.

Do NOT build:

* A generic AI chatbot
* A full claims-management system
* A static dashboard
* An autonomous claim decision-maker

Prioritize one excellent workflow:

**Dashboard → risky claim → what changed → why → preventative intervention**

* Repeated contacts
* Outstanding assessments
* Customer sentiment

A transparent rules-based scoring model prioritizes claims.
